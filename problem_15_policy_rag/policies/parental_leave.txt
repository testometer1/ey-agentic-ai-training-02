# Parental leave policy
# Document: POL-HR-PAR-2026  |  Section 3 Paid leave

Effective 1 April 2026.

Section 3.1 Qualifying event
Parental leave covers 12 weeks of paid leave after the birth, adoption, or placement of a child.
The 12 weeks must start within 12 months of the qualifying event.

Section 3.2 Eligibility
Employees with at least six months of continuous service may take the leave.
The policy applies equally to birthing and non-birthing parents.

Section 3.3 Out of scope
This document does not grant pet insurance, gym membership, or company cars.
Short-term disability is described in POL-HR-STD, which is not part of this extract.
