# Vacation and time-off policy
# Document: POL-HR-VAC-2026  |  Section 2.1 New hires

Effective 1 January 2026.

Section 2.1 New hires
Employees in their first calendar year receive 20 vacation days, prorated from the hire month.
Unused first-year days do not carry over except as required by local law.

Section 2.2 Existing staff
From the second calendar year, full-time staff receive 25 vacation days.

Section 2.3 Approval
Vacation longer than five consecutive days needs written approval from the line manager.
This document does not set public-holiday calendars; see POL-HR-HOL.
