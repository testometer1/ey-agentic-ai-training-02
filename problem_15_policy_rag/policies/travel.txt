# Travel and expense policy
# Document: POL-HR-TRV-2026  |  Section 4 Expense submission

Effective 1 March 2026.

Section 4.1 Submission window
Travel expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the return date.
Claims after 30 days are rejected unless Finance grants a written exception.

Section 4.2 What can be expensed
Air, rail, hotel, and client meals are allowed when pre-approved on the trip request.
Alcohol is reimbursable only when entertaining a client and itemised on the receipt.

Section 4.3 What this policy does not cover
This policy does not describe payroll, equity, or benefits enrolment.
Local transport under 15 USD may be claimed without a receipt if the date and route are noted.
